Study tools

Ways to check what you know and find what you do not. They go from gentle (answers right away) to demanding (a timed exam). Everything runs in your browser, and your progress is stored only there.

  • Learn as you go

    Quiz questions

    Pick modules and answer questions with instant feedback and an explanation.

  • Memorize terms

    Flashcards

    Glossary cards that return more often until you know them.

  • Apply ideas

    Scenario questions

    Which scheduler, lock, isolation or operating system fits this situation, and why?

  • Do the math

    Calculation practice

    A fresh scheduling, paging, disk or deadlock problem each time, with worked solutions.

  • Test yourself

    Exam mode

    A timed, shuffled exam with no hints, a score by module, and a review of every miss.

  • Keep track

    My progress

    Modules visited, questions mastered and exam scores. Private, and exportable.
